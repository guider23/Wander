# Privacy Policy for Wander

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Wander is an open-source productivity and attention-tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have full control over your data.

## 1. Data Storage and Local-First Architecture
Wander is built with a strictly local-first architecture. All of your data (tasks, thoughts, history, and settings) is stored locally on your device by default. We do not operate central servers that collect, store, or process your personal notes or tasks. 

## 2. Google Drive Backup Integration
Wander offers an optional cloud backup feature using Google Drive. If you choose to enable this feature, please be aware of the following:

- **Restricted Scope of Access:** Wander requests access *only* to a specific, hidden application data folder (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata` scope).
- **No Access to Personal Files:** Wander cannot read, modify, delete, or even see any of your personal files, documents, or photos stored in your Google Drive. It is restricted entirely to the files it creates itself within its dedicated, sandboxed app data folder.
- **Data Transfer:** Your backup data is transmitted securely via standard encryption protocols (HTTPS) directly between your device and Google's servers.

## 3. Analytics and Telemetry
Wander does not include any tracking, telemetry, or analytics software. Your usage habits, attention spans, and tasks are your business alone and remain on your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Wander uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API strictly to facilitate the cloud backup functionality. Your authentication and use of these specific cloud services are subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Open Source Transparency
Wander is open-source software. You can verify exactly how the application handles your data, authenticates with Google, and performs backups by inspecting the source code in our repository.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
